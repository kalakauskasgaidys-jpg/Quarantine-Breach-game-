# Epic: quarantine-breach

**Goal:** Lead squads into procedurally generated towns overrun by the dead to pull out survivors and supplies before the chopper leaves, where every gunshot calls the horde and every bite turns your own people against you, all while the infection eats the region sector by sector.

**Source:** `design/game-brief.md` (MVP, Build order)

**Scope (MVP):**
1. Procedural suburb
2. Squad control and loadouts
3. Noise-driven horde with time of day
4. Infection turning
5. Extraction mission
6. Call-in support that grows with equipment
7. Region campaign map

**Build order:** greybox squad vs horde (stories 001–002) → infection (003) → full mission (004) → procedural suburb (005) → call-ins (006) → campaign map (007)
